We are happy to announce that the [Third UK meeting on enabling AI in HEP experiments and theory](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1622413/) will take place at Imperial College, London, on 26 March 2026!

We expect to include contributions/discussion in areas such as:

- Briefings on:
  - STFC/UKRI AI strategy
  - ECFA AI strategy
  - Funding opportunities beyond STFC
- Discussion of key topics in terms of enabling AI for HEP (barriers/needs/opportunities):
  - Software
  - AI/ML-Ops
  - Knowledge exchange within and beyond HEP
  - Industry and wider engagement
  - Other issues/challenges

See you there!
